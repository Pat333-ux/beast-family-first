# BEAST FAMILY FIRST PREDICTIVE INTERVENTION ENGINE

## Purpose

The Predictive Intervention Engine identifies elevated-risk conditions before a household enters crisis.

Its objective is to move government response from reactive assistance to proactive stabilization.

---

# Mission

Detect

Predict

Intervene

Prevent

Stabilize

---

# Core Principle

The best crisis is the one that never occurs.

The system continuously monitors household stability indicators and recommends support before emergency conditions emerge.
