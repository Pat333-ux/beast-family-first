# BEAST FAMILY FIRST SYSTEM WORKFLOW ENGINE

## Purpose

Define the operational workflow that governs every Family First case from intake through long-term stabilization.

---

ASSESSMENT

---

# Stage 1: Intake

Objective:

Capture household needs through a single application.

Inputs:

- Household Size
- Housing Status
- Employment Status
- Food Security
- Mental Health Indicators
- Household Stress Indicators
- Child Wellbeing Indicators

Output:

Family Intake Record

Status:

INTAKE_COMPLETE

---

# Stage 2: Assessment

Objective:

Evaluate household conditions.

Assessment Categories:

Housing

Food Security

Employment

Mental Health

Household Stress

Child Wellbeing

Output:

Assessment Record

Status:

ASSESSMENT_COMPLETE

---

# Stage 3: Stability Scoring

Objective:

Generate an explainable family stability score.

Formula:

Housing × 15%

Food × 10%

Employment × 20%

Mental Health × 20%

Household Stress × 15%

Child Wellbeing × 20%

Output:

0-100 Family Stability Score

Status:

SCORE_COMPLETE

---

# Stage 4: Risk Classification

---

# Stage 6: Human Review

Objective:

Maintain accountability.

Review Functions:

- Approve
- Decline
- Escalate
- Request Information
- Override

Output:

Review Decision

Status:

APPROVED

or

DECLINED

or

ESCALATED

---

# Stage 7: Assignment

Objective:

Connect family to resources.

Assignments:

- Housing Programs
- Food Assistance
- Employment Services
- Wellness Services
- Community Partners

Output:

Assignment Records

Status:

ASSIGNED
