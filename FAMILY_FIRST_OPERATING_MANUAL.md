# FAMILY FIRST OPERATING MANUAL

## BEAST FAMILY FIRST GOVERNMENT PLATFORM

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This manual establishes standard operating procedures for all personnel, partners, administrators, and stakeholders operating within the BEAST Family First Platform.

The objective is to ensure every family receives timely, consistent, transparent, and accountable support.

---

# Program Mission

Protect Families

Prevent Crisis

Promote Stability

Support Recovery

Improve Outcomes

Strengthen Communities
