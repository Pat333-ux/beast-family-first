# BEAST FAMILY FIRST AI DECISION SUPPORT FRAMEWORK

## Project

BEAST Family First Government Platform

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This framework defines how Artificial Intelligence may assist staff, families, supervisors, and administrators while ensuring that human review remains responsible for all final decisions.

AI exists to support.

Humans remain accountable.

---

# Core Mission

Analyze

Recommend

Explain

Support

Audit

Improve

---

# Guiding Principle

AI SHALL NOT:

- Replace human judgment
- Approve benefits
- Deny services
- Determine legal rights
- Remove human oversight

AI MAY:

- Analyze data
- Detect patterns
- Prioritize reviews
- Recommend resources
- Generate summaries
- Assist decision-makers

---

# Decision Hierarchy

Family
↓

Case Worker

↓

Supervisor

↓

# AI Responsibilities

## Assessment Assistance

Review assessment inputs.

Highlight:

- Missing information
- Inconsistent information
- Potential risk indicators

Output:

Recommendations

Not decisions.

---

## Scoring Assistance

Calculate:

Housing Score

Food Score

Employment Score

Mental Health Score

Household Stress Score

Child Wellbeing Score

Generate:

Family Stability Score

Explain calculation.

---

## Routing Assistance

Recommend:

Housing Programs

Food Resources

Mental Health Resources

Employment Services

Family Stabilization Services

Generate explanation.

---

## Monitoring Assistance

Track:

Case Activity

Progress Trends

Risk Indicators

Recommendation Effectiveness

Alert staff to significant changes.

# Explainability Requirements

Every recommendation must answer:

What happened?

Why was this recommendation generated?

What data influenced the recommendation?

Which rules were triggered?

What alternatives were considered?
