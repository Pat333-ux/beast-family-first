# Operations and Implementation

## Four-Step Operating Cycle

1. Assess
2. Stabilize
3. Heal
4. Thrive

## Initial Rollout Phases

- Phase 1: Foundation (intake + scoring)
- Phase 2: Routing and stabilization services
- Phase 3: Trauma and mental health pathways
- Phase 4: Predictive intervention and optimization

## Operational Priorities

- Reduce time-to-support
- Increase prevention outcomes
- Improve coordination across agencies
