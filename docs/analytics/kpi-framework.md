# Outcome Analytics and KPI Framework

## Core KPIs

- Time to intervention
- Evictions prevented
- Homelessness prevention rate
- Family stability score improvement (30/90/180 day)
- Mental health referral and engagement rates
- Child wellbeing continuity indicators
- Cost avoidance and budget efficiency

## Dashboard Domains

- Family stability
- Program throughput
- Service engagement
- Preventive outcomes
- Fiscal impact
