# Implementation Roadmap

## Near-Term (0-90 Days)

- Finalize architecture and governance documents
- Define pilot jurisdictions and partner agencies
- Stand up intake and baseline scoring workflow

## Mid-Term (3-9 Months)

- Launch automated routing and dashboard reporting
- Integrate trauma prevention and mental health pathways
- Begin predictive indicator testing

## Long-Term (9-24 Months)

- Scale to statewide/federal-aligned deployment
- Optimize budget and outcomes using analytics
- Institutionalize continuous improvement cycles
